# Tesla Model Y is the better overall choice for a 100-car sales fleet

**Subtitle:** Stronger range, charging convenience, cargo space, and lower routine-maintenance burden make the Model Y the best overall fleet fit; the Mustang Mach-E remains the practical alternative when dealer support, BlueCruise, or brand familiarity matter.

- **Recommended model: Tesla Model Y** for the best overall operational efficiency in a 100-car sales fleet.
- **Why it leads:** longer range, faster charging convenience via the Tesla Supercharger network and native North American charging standard, and more cargo space.
- **Fleet advantage:** Tesla says the Model Y does not require annual maintenance or regular fluid changes, helping reduce downtime and simplify fleet management.
- **Where the Mach-E fits:** Ford Mustang Mach-E is a solid alternative for teams that value Ford dealer support, a familiar brand, and available BlueCruise hands-free highway assistance.
- **Decision takeaway:** if the priority is productivity and charging convenience, choose Model Y; if dealer service relationships or BlueCruise matter more, choose Mach-E.

**Speaker note:** For a 100-car sales fleet, the decision comes down to operational efficiency. The Tesla Model Y offers the stronger overall package for range, charging, cargo, and routine-maintenance simplicity. The Ford Mustang Mach-E remains a credible alternative when dealer support, BlueCruise, or a more familiar Ford-backed service model is the priority.
