# Banoffee Pie (summary of Sally’s Baking Addiction recipe)

**Source:** https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/banoffee-pie/

## What you’re making
A no-bake-style layered pie (with a baked crust): **graham cracker crust**, **dulce de leche**, **sliced bananas**, and **whipped cream**.

## Ingredients (key amounts)
### Graham cracker crust
- 1 1/2 cups (180g) graham cracker crumbs
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 6 tbsp (85g) unsalted butter, melted

### Filling
- 1 1/4 cups dulce de leche
- 2 large bananas, sliced

### Whipped cream
- Heavy cream (whipped with sugar + vanilla)

## Method (high level)
1. **Make crust:** Mix graham crumbs + sugar; stir in melted butter. Press into an ungreased pie dish (bottom and up the sides).
2. **Make dulce de leche:** Use store-bought, or make by baking sweetened condensed milk in a water bath until thick and caramelized (about 2 hours at 350°F/177°C).
3. **Assemble:** Spread dulce de leche over the crust, layer banana slices, then spoon/spread whipped cream on top.
4. **Chill:** Refrigerate **at least 2 hours** (up to 1 day). Serve cold; keep leftovers refrigerated.
